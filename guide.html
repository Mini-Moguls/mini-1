Choosing your product: turning ideas into things people love

How Junior Moguls (5–8*), Young Moguls (9–13) and Senior Moguls (14–18) can brainstorm a great idea, price creations clearly and get ready for Mini Moguls Market Day on Saturday, 12 December 2026 in Harare.

Every young entrepreneur begins with an idea. Mini Moguls Market Day creates an opportunity for children and young people in Harare to explore their creativity, share what they can make and experience the excitement of turning an idea into something real.

Big ideas start somewhere

Through creating, showcasing and selling their ideas, young participants can explore entrepreneurship, develop confidence and discover practical lessons that extend beyond the classroom. The best stall ideas don’t need complicated equipment — they begin with curiosity and something a young creator genuinely enjoys making.

Whether it is handmade jewellery, illustrated bookmarks, home-baked treats, upcycled planters or a table-top game, Market Day is all about learning by doing.

Choosing what to make

When brainstorming a product for Saturday, 12 December 2026, encourage young creators to focus on one clear idea they can make well and repeat comfortably:

  • CREATE: Bring ideas to life through original products, creative projects and innovative thinking.
  • SELL: Showcase your creations, connect with potential customers and discover the basics of running a small business.
  • LEARN: Explore entrepreneurship, money management, communication and the value of learning by doing.
  • TEST IT FIRST: Make a small sample batch for family and friends to see how long each item takes to finish.

Explore all six showcase categories on our Events & Categories page to see where your idea fits best.

“The best first business isn’t the most complicated one — it’s the idea you are proud to explain with a smile.”

Pricing & preparation

Learning how money works is one of the most rewarding parts of Market Day. Young creators should write down every material they use so they can see what each item costs to make before choosing a selling price.

Keep prices in simple, round amounts so it is easy for visitors to pay and easy for young entrepreneurs to count change quickly during the 10:00 AM–4:00 PM market session.

A simple preparation plan

  • Four weeks before: finalise your product idea and test a sample batch at home.
  • Two weeks before: prepare your stock, price labels and table sign.
  • The week before: pack your display box and practise your welcome greeting.

Market Day checklist & organiser confirmations

While the specific Harare venue, participant registration fee (if any), registration deadline, and whether tables, stalls or equipment are provided by the organisers are to be confirmed, here is a helpful general checklist of personal items young creators typically prepare (note: age eligibility is listed as 5–18 in the concept note across Junior Moguls 5–8, Young Moguls 9–13, and Senior Moguls 14–18, and 6–18 on the promotional poster, flagged for organiser confirmation):

Creator’s checklist

  • Your finished products & creations
  • Clear handwritten price tags
  • A bold stall name sign
  • Small notebook & pens for sales
  • Cash box or pouch for small change
  • Tablecloth or simple display stand
  • Drinking water & snacks for the day
  • Paper bags or eco-friendly wrapping
  • A short story card about how you made it
  • Your biggest smile & confidence

Connecting with customers

Visitors come to Mini Moguls Market Day because they want to support young creators and hear the story behind each idea. A few simple habits make a big difference:

  • Greet visitors warmly when they stop to look at your table.
  • Share what inspired your product and how you made it.
  • Make sure every price is clearly visible so customers feel comfortable browsing.
  • Thank every visitor — even if they are just looking and encouraging you.
