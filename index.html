Introduction & Showcase Preview

Where little ideas become big possibilities.

Mini Moguls Market Day gives children and young people a real-world marketplace where they can create original products, present their ideas to visitors and discover the excitement of entrepreneurship by doing.

Core Experience & Event Details

CREATE. SELL. LEARN.

Every young entrepreneur begins with an idea. Rather than learning only from a textbook, Mini Moguls Market Day invites children and young people to step behind their own market table, share what they have made and build lifelong confidence.

Why Mini Moguls & Age Groups

Three age groups, one inspiring marketplace

Participation is structured around three age groups so every child and teenager can create, sell and learn alongside peers at a similar stage.

Register Your Child

*Age-range confirmation note: The event concept note defines the three groups as Junior Moguls (5–8), Young Moguls (9–13) and Senior Moguls (14–18) (ages 5–18 overall), whereas the promotional poster states ages 6–18. Final starting age eligibility (5 vs 6 years) is flagged for organiser confirmation — please contact the team if registering a 5-year-old participant.
