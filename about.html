Our Vision & Purpose

Why introduce children to entrepreneurship?

Children naturally love to imagine, build and invent. When they are given the space to shape an idea into a real product and share it with others, they discover that their creativity has value — and that they are capable of solving problems, communicating clearly and taking initiative.

  1. What It Is

    A Dedicated Marketplace for Young Creators

    Mini Moguls Market Day is a curated one-day event in Harare on Saturday, 12 December 2026 (10:00 AM–4:00 PM) where children and young people step into the role of creators, sellers and young entrepreneurs.

  2. The Vision

    Nurturing Creativity & Self-Belief Early

    Our vision is to cultivate a culture where young people don’t just consume products — they understand how ideas are made, how value is created and how their own talents can grow into opportunities.

  3. Practical Learning

    Learning by Doing, Not Just Listening

    Some lessons can only be learned through real participation: planning a small batch, deciding on a fair price, arranging a table, looking a customer in the eye and counting real change.

  4. What We Hope to Achieve

    Confident, Capable Young Thinkers

    The organisers aim to spark lasting confidence, financial awareness, communication skills and creative pride — bringing families, schools and local businesses together around Zimbabwe’s next generation.

Who the Event Is Designed For

Built for young creators, families & partners

Mini Moguls Market Day brings together three participant age groups alongside supportive parents, educators and community sponsors.

  • Junior Moguls

    Concept Note: Ages 5–8*

    Younger children taking their very first steps into making simple crafts, sharing their creations with visitors and discovering how a market works with parent or guardian support.

  • Young Moguls

    Concept Note: Ages 9–13

    Confident young makers ready to plan a product idea, prepare inventory, design price signs, arrange their stall display and interact with customers directly.

  • Senior Moguls

    Concept Note: Ages 14–18

    Teen entrepreneurs and innovators showcasing original brands, handmade lines, baked goods, digital projects or sustainable products while practising real business skills.

  • Parents & Guardians

    Registration & Encouragement

    Parents and guardians submit registration on behalf of their children, encourage preparation at home and celebrate their children’s growth on Market Day.

  • Families & Visitors

    Harare Community Supporters

    Friends, relatives and visitors whose questions, encouragement and purchases make every young creator’s market-day experience memorable and rewarding.

  • Sponsors & Partners

    Businesses & Organisations

    Forward-thinking companies, schools and organisations investing in youth entrepreneurship, financial literacy, creative learning and community development.

Guiding Values

The values that guide Mini Moguls Market Day

Every part of the experience is shaped around six core values that celebrate young people as capable thinkers, makers and communicators.

  • Creativity & innovation

    Encouraging children to imagine original products, experiment with materials and bring fresh ideas to life.

  • Entrepreneurship from a young age

    Introducing business thinking in an age-appropriate, practical and inspiring marketplace environment.

  • Confidence & self-expression

    Helping young creators take pride in their work, express their individuality and share their ideas openly.

  • Financial awareness

    Building a practical understanding of costs, fair pricing, sales, saving and the value of effort.

  • Communication & customer care

    Practising warm greetings, clear explanations, active listening and respectful interaction with visitors.

  • Problem-solving & collaboration

    Working together with peers and family, adapting on the day and learning through real-world experience.
