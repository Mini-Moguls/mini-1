FAQ

Good questions

Clear, accurate answers for parents, guardians, young creators, visitors and sponsors.

Who can participate as a young mogul, and what are the age groups?

According to the event concept note, participants are grouped into three age categories: Junior Moguls (Ages 5–8), Young Moguls (Ages 9–13), and Senior Moguls (Ages 14–18). Important note for parents: The promotional poster states ages 6–18, while the concept note states ages 5–18 (Junior Moguls 5–8). This minimum-age difference (5 vs 6) is flagged for organiser confirmation — please call +263 771 222 748 or +263 783 116 564 if registering a 5-year-old.

What kind of products can children and young people showcase?

Showcases span six core areas: Arts, Crafts & Design, Baking, Food & Treats, Tech & Digital Innovation, Fashion & Accessories, Games & Interactive Fun, and Green & Sustainable Ideas. Creations should be safe, age-appropriate and made, designed or assembled by the young participants.

How does registration work, and what details are still to be confirmed?

A parent or legal guardian must submit registration on behalf of a child. Organisers will contact you to confirm participation. Please note that the participant registration fee (if any), registration deadline, payment methods, and whether stalls, tables or equipment are provided vs brought by participants are currently to be confirmed by the organisers.

When and where will Mini Moguls Market Day take place?

Mini Moguls Market Day takes place on Saturday, 12 December 2026, from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in Harare, Zimbabwe. The specific venue in Harare and the detailed on-the-day programme schedule are to be announced.

What is the entrance fee for visitors and the general public?

The general public entrance fee is US$5 per person, subject to final confirmation. Parents, relatives, friends and community members across Harare are warmly invited to attend, encourage the young creators and buy their products.

How can sponsors, partners and businesses get involved?

We welcome conversations with businesses, schools, financial institutions and community partners interested in event sponsorship, category support, educational mentorship or starter materials. Specific sponsorship packages are open for discussion directly with the organising team on +263 771 222 748 or +263 783 116 564.
