Dear Partner,

We are delighted to invite your organisation to partner with Mini Moguls Market Day, taking place on Saturday, 12 December 2026 from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM at the National Botanic Gardens in Harare, Zimbabwe.

Built around the tagline CREATE. SELL. LEARN., Mini Moguls Market Day brings children and young people across three age tracks—Junior Moguls (Ages 5–8), Young Moguls (Ages 9–13), and Senior Moguls (Ages 14–18)—together to create original products, run their own market stalls, interact with customers and develop practical financial literacy and entrepreneurial confidence.

We welcome partnerships across our six creative Showcase Zones (Arts, Crafts & Design, Baking, Food & Treats, Tech & Digital Innovation, Fashion & Accessories, Games & Interactive Fun, and Green & Sustainable Ideas), as well as youth awards, starter kits and event infrastructure support.

We would love to schedule a brief call or meeting to tailor a partnership that aligns with your brand and community goals. You can reach our organising team directly at partners@minimoguls.online or on +263 771 222 748 / +263 783 116 564.
