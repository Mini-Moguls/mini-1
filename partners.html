Why partner with Mini Moguls Market Day?

Partnering with Mini Moguls Market Day connects your organisation with families, educators and young creators across Harare while directly supporting youth entrepreneurship, financial literacy, confidence and practical learning by doing.

  • • Support youth entrepreneurship & creativity
  • • Promote practical financial awareness
  • • Invest in future community leaders
  • • Connect positively with Harare families
